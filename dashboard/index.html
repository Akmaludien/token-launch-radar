How the radar works

The value model

Implied value = TVL × multiple × growth × maturity × breadth.

  • Multiple fades with size: 0.30 at $50M TVL and below, 0.15 at $1B and above, log-interpolated between. Value per dollar locked compresses as a protocol grows.
  • Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%..+120%, at half weight.
  • Maturity runs 0.60 to 1.00 over three years tracked with no token; unknown age counts as one year.
  • Breadth adds 2% per extra chain, up to +10% for six or more chains.

What counts as pre-token

  • The snapshot records no token: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol.
  • Not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, not an exchange, not a versioned pool. The watchlist’s 40 are the floor; the early finds below it pass the same screen.
  • A protocol at or above the floor that the watchlist leaves out stays out: the house’s rule saw a parent protocol the snapshot’s ten fields cannot show.
  • “Records no token” is not proof it has none — a token may exist that the record does not carry.

Reading the farm plans

  • Each plan is six steps with a cost and a time, derived from the protocol’s category, chains, TVL and age.
  • Nothing here promises a token. The plan puts the activity on record if one ever comes.
  • Never pay to claim, never connect a wallet from a link you did not open yourself, and farm with money you can afford to leave in the position.
